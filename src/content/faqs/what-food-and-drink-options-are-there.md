Food and drink vendors will be serving from 5pm until 9pm, you can find a range of food vendors in the park (including Crumpy's Smoke Shack, The Vegan Street Diner, Ladda Thai Cuisine, The German Grill, Hey Spud, Dirty Vurgerz, Lemon Stone, Korean Corndog Station, Coffee Can, Candylicious Catering, Churros Ole Ltd and Bootleg Bars for the bar and mulled wine). There are also additional food vendors in the car park at Birdies.
