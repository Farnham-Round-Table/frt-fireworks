---
question: "How do I get involved in helping to run the fireworks?"
order: 240
---

Farnham Round Table is a social group open to men aged 18-45, get in touch and come meet the group at one of our upcoming socials (everything from axe throwing to go-karting): https://farnhamroundtable.org.uk/about-us/contact/. If you are part of a community group that wants to get involved in helping the fireworks be a success, do get in touch and we discuss what opportunities exist.
